F.A.Q

Straightforward answers, so you can move your fleet forward with confidence.

01What does Velarc do?

We design and build battery-electric trucks, install and run the chargers they need, and operate the software that keeps a fleet moving, all under one contract.

02Which routes suit an electric truck?

Port shuttles, hub-to-hub distribution and long haul up to 850 km between charges. We run a free route study on your real telematics before you commit.

03How long does charging take?

About 32 minutes from 10 to 80% on a 1.2 MW Arc Bay, roughly a mandated driver break. Overnight depot charging fills a pack completely.

04Can I keep my existing trailers?

Yes. T-80 and R-40 tractors use a standard fifth wheel and work with any compliant semi-trailer, reefers included.

05How is pricing calculated?

Buy outright, lease, or choose a per-kilometre plan that bundles truck, energy, charging and service into one rate.

06What happens if a truck breaks down?

Fleet OS alerts our uptime desk before the driver calls, and a technician or replacement tractor reaches you within four hours on every corridor we serve.

07How hot is too hot?

Packs are liquid-cooled and validated at 55 °C ambient. Our range figures are rated at 45 °C with full cabin cooling.

08How do I reserve a truck?

Leave your email below and a fleet engineer will set up a test drive and a route study. Current lead time for the T-80 is about 14 weeks.
